Application overview

Personal Paper Reader

A private tool for one user to organize academic PDFs and read them more comfortably on a mobile device.

What it does

  • Checks a user-selected Google Drive folder for research PDFs.
  • Renames and moves papers between user-selected folders.
  • Uploads user-requested mobile reading exports.
  • Keeps processing and duplicate-detection records for the user.

Who can use it

The application is private and non-commercial. Access to its reader and processing interface is restricted to its owner.

Google Drive access

Google Drive access is used only to provide the file-management features initiated or configured by the user. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
